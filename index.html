What you're still not charged for

  • Inbound messages. Anything the customer sends you is free.
  • Your first 1,000 service messages a month, per WhatsApp Business Account, resetting monthly.
  • Click-to-WhatsApp ad conversations. Free today, and Meta is extending the window to 7 days — date still to be confirmed.

Changed on 1 October 2026: service messages are billable per message, and utility templates sent inside the 24-hour window are billable again. Both were free before.

What's assumed here, so you can check it.
  1. Plan prices, extra-user prices and add-on prices are the published India figures. Per-message rates come from the Wati usage rate card that applies to this region, effective 1 July 2026, and are charged on the recipient's country. Each plan tier has its own rates — the higher tiers are cheaper per message.
  2. Plan prices and per-message rates are both in INR, so no conversion is involved. The one exception is the Shopify integration, billed in USD at $4.99 a month and converted at today's rate. Service messages have their own per-country rate, lower than the utility rate.
  3. The rate cards this calculator is built from are linked under question 1, so you can open the source document on a call rather than quoting from memory.
  4. The estimate picks the cheapest plan that meets your requirements, but you can pin a specific plan in the receipt to quote a higher tier. If a pinned plan can't cover the requirements entered, the costs it can't carry are left out of the total and flagged above it.
  5. Growth's 15,000-broadcast monthly cap is checked against marketing plus utility volume. Authentication messages are API-triggered one-to-one and aren't treated as broadcasts. Pro and Business have no broadcast cap.
  6. Included automation triggers: 1,000 on Growth, 2,000 on Pro, 5,000 on Business. Top-ups are sold per 1,000 and the estimate rounds up to whole blocks. Unused triggers don't roll over.
  7. Included Wati AI credits: 2,000 on Growth, 5,000 on Pro, 20,000 on Business, as one pool shared between Wati AI and Astra. Top-ups are sold in blocks of 2,000 — the smallest pack you can buy — and the estimate rounds up to whole blocks. Credits expire on your billing date each month and do not roll over, and neither do unused top-ups.
  8. Astra AI agents are included with the plan: 1 on Growth, 2 on Pro, unlimited on Business, with Voice AI on Business only. They are not a separate subscription and are not priced here.
  9. Extra WhatsApp numbers need the Business plan. Shopify is billed in USD at $4.99 a month everywhere, so it moves with the exchange rate in other currencies.
  10. Message charges are drawn from a prepaid wallet, separate from the subscription invoice.
  11. Business volume discounts above roughly a million messages a month aren't modelled. Also excluded: Astra AI agents, dedicated onboarding, technical account management, professional services, green-tick verification and the Blitz high-throughput add-on — all quoted separately.
  12. Exchange rates come from Frankfurter, falling back to Exchange Rate API. Both publish once a day, so the rate is a daily reference rate, not a dealing rate. Wati invoices in INR; your bank's rate on the day will differ slightly.
