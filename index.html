What you're never charged for

  • Service conversations. A customer writes in, you reply — free on Growth, Pro and Business alike.
  • Click-to-WhatsApp ad conversations. Chats opened from a CTWA ad are free.
  • Utility replies inside the 24-hour service window. Set by the slider above.

What's assumed here, so you can check it.
  1. Plan prices, extra-user prices and add-on prices are the published India figures. Per-message rates come from the Wati usage rate card that applies to this region, effective 1 July 2026, and are charged on the recipient's country. Each plan tier has its own rates — the higher tiers are cheaper per message.
  2. Plan prices and per-message rates are both in INR, so no conversion is involved. The one exception is the Shopify integration, billed in USD at $4.99 a month and converted at today's rate.
  3. Growth's 15,000-broadcast monthly cap is checked against marketing plus utility volume. Authentication messages are API-triggered one-to-one and aren't treated as broadcasts. Pro and Business have no broadcast cap.
  4. Included automation triggers: 1,000 on Growth, 2,000 on Pro, 5,000 on Business. Top-ups are sold per 1,000 and the estimate rounds up to whole blocks. Unused triggers don't roll over.
  5. Included AI Co-pilot credits: 250 on Growth, 500 on Pro, 1,500 on Business. Top-ups are sold per 1,000.
  6. Extra WhatsApp numbers need the Business plan. Shopify is billed in USD at $4.99 a month everywhere, so it moves with the exchange rate in other currencies.
  7. Message charges are drawn from a prepaid wallet, separate from the subscription invoice.
  8. Business volume discounts above roughly a million messages a month aren't modelled. Also excluded: Astra AI agents, dedicated onboarding, technical account management, professional services, green-tick verification and the Blitz high-throughput add-on — all quoted separately.
  9. Exchange rates come from Frankfurter, falling back to Exchange Rate API. Both publish once a day, so the rate is a daily reference rate, not a dealing rate. Wati invoices in INR; your bank's rate on the day will differ slightly.
